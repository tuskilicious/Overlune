# Changelog

What changed in each Overlune release. Overlay links from every release keep working: the link format only ever gains optional settings.

## 2.2.0 (2026-10-20)

Four new looks, each with its own crescent moon, and a page for every look. Every existing link still works. There is nothing to re-paste.

### New looks
- **Eclipse:** a gold corona over a quiet observatory rail.
- **Rain City:** a crescent sign over a wet street, last train home.
- **Paper Cut:** a crescent window cut through layered plum and white paper.
- **Aurora Tundra:** a frost crescent above a layered polar ice shelf.
- Each one comes with all six scenes, chat, alerts with its own sound, the webcam frame and Live, horizontal and vertical. That makes 20 looks.

### A page for every look
- **See a look before you pick it:** overlune.in/looks/rain-city and the rest show the look's Starting Soon in 16:9 and vertical, what comes with it, and its fonts and colors (with their codes, to match your panels and emotes).
- **"Use" picks the look:** the button on a look's page, and the one under the home page's looks, open the editor with that look picked. A saved overlay keeps its text and layout.
- **The home page's looks** each open their own page.

### Site
- **Shows up faster:** the home page, the setup guide, privacy, terms and the look pages arrive ready to read, before the app loads. Search engines can read them too, and a sitemap lists every page.
- **Pages a link opens start at the top,** not where the last page was scrolled to.
- **New support email:** overlune@proton.me.
- **Fixes:** Rain City's countdown units sit between its tiles, the editor's preview controls stay on one row, and the browser no longer logs an error for every signed-out visitor.

## 2.1.0 (2026-10-15)

Every existing link still works. Your overlays now fit a vertical stream too, with two new scenes and a smoother time in OBS. There is nothing to re-paste for your current setup.

### Vertical overlays

### New scenes

### Editor

- **Show safe areas and thirds:** guides help you place things clear of the edges and the apps' controls.
- **Star on GitHub:** a link in the navigation if you want to support the project.

### Smoother in OBS
- **Lighter effects:** entrances no longer blur, Vaporwave Sunset's sun stripes hold still, and Session's grain no longer blends across the scene.
- **Entrances replay** when OBS shows a scene live again.

### Site
- **Light or dark:** choose the site's mode from the navigation, with new icons, buttons and a matching toggle.
- **Clearer on phones:** a note explains that Overlune is made for a computer, and mobile editor fixes keep the preview and controls usable.

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-14)

Move anything, anywhere: a canvas editor for every scene, a Live overlay that puts chat, alerts and your webcam frame in one source, and any canvas size. It's a big new version, but every link you've already pasted still works exactly as before: customizing is optional, and only links you copy after customizing carry a layout.

### Canvas editor
- **Customize any scene:** press Customize above the preview, then drag, resize and turn the title, countdown, logo, socials and ticker. Parts snap to the edges, the middle and each other, and each look starts from its own layout, so nothing jumps.
- **Layers and Inspector:** hide, lock and reorder parts, and set an exact place, size, turn, see-through and colors.
- **Add your own:** text, shapes and pictures (https links), up to 40 things per scene. Delete a part of the look and "Put back" returns it.
- **Reset to the look** puts a scene back, and Undo takes back a whole drag.
- **By keyboard too:** Tab picks a part, the arrow keys move it, Alt resizes it, and a "Keyboard keys" list shows them all. Screen readers hear what's picked and where.

### Live overlay
- **One source for going live:** the new Live link shows chat, alerts and the webcam frame together, each in a box you can move on the canvas. Use it instead of those three, or keep them separate as before.

### Canvas sizes
- **Your OBS canvas size:** pick 1280×720, 2560×1440, 4K or your own size next to the preview, and the scenes and Live fill it exactly. Your layout moves over with it.

### Alerts
- **Follow alerts in every look.** Follows need you to sign in with Twitch, and start showing in a coming update.

### Site
- **One site:** the night sky and the same navigation on every page; the setup guide, privacy policy and terms with an index that follows your place.
- **A look gallery** on your first visit to the editor.
- **The guide covers customizing,** with the canvas keys.
- **Fixes:** the account menu stays closed until you open it, and text meant only for screen readers no longer shows on the editor and guide.

## 1.6.0 (2026-10-13)

Sign in with Twitch, if you want to. Everything still works without an account, and overlays and their links are unchanged: there is nothing to re-paste.

### Accounts
- **Sign in with Twitch:** a button in the editor's header. You sign in on Twitch's own page, and Overlune asks for no Twitch permissions, only who you are. Your Twitch name and picture then show in the editor.
- **Your account, your call:** from the account menu, sign out, sign out on every device, or delete your account, which removes it at once. Your overlay links keep working either way.
- This is the groundwork: saving overlays to your account comes later.

### Privacy and safety
- **A rewritten privacy policy and terms** say what an account stores (your Twitch ID, name and picture link, and your sign-ins), what it never stores (your password, email or chat), how long it's kept, and how to delete it.
- **Two cookies, only if you sign in,** both needed to keep you signed in. Still no tracking cookies, and no cookie banner.
- **Limits against abuse,** and **nightly encrypted backups**, kept for 90 days.

### Privacy
- **Fixed:** your overlay settings could reach our error reports. When an overlay loaded, the error tracker's performance sample (taken for about 1 in 20 loads) stored the full link, settings included. Now every field of every report is cleaned before it's sent, and a report is dropped rather than sent if cleaning ever fails. A second rule in the error tracker removes settings on its side too.
- The site's source maps are no longer public. They go only to the error tracker, so its reports point at the right file and line.

### Fixed
- The alert pictures on the home page crop to their cards again.

### Under the hood
- Updated React Router (8), Vite (8), Vitest (5), TypeScript (6.0) and Sentry (11), keeping exactly the same data settings and scrubbing as before.
- The second copy at overlune.antideploy.app has been retired. overlune.in is the only address.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-03)

The first release. Free, open source, no account and no payment.

### What you can make
- **Scenes:** Starting Soon (with a countdown, one-off or repeating on set days), Be Right Back and Stream Ending.
- **Chat:** live Twitch chat with emotes, role badges (can be hidden), bot and command filters, your own size, text size and fade-out time.
- **Alerts:** raids, subs, resubs, gift subs (grouped when someone gifts many) and bits, with sound, your own messages, volume and how long each alert stays up.
- **8 looks:** Clean Slate, Neon Grid, Cozy Café, Arcade 8-Bit, Pastel Cloud, Forest Night, Bold Esports and Vaporwave Sunset. Every scene, chat and alert in a look matches. Advanced colors and fonts on top.
- **Your logo and socials** on the scenes (the logo comes from an image link; uploads aren't supported yet).

### How it works
- One link per overlay to paste into OBS or Streamlabs as a Browser source. The link holds your settings, so there's nothing to sign up for, and it never changes when a look gets an update.
- The editor shows a live preview, saves your work in your browser, and gives you a link that reopens it anywhere. "Copy all links" copies every link with its size.
- A step-by-step setup guide for OBS and Streamlabs, with fixes for a black box, empty chat, silent alerts and a wrong size.
- A "Less motion" option, and every animation has a calm version for viewers and streamers who prefer reduced motion.
- Test alerts in the editor, and a test link that plays one of each alert in OBS.

### Known limits
- Chat and alerts work with **Twitch only**. The scenes work anywhere, including YouTube.
- No follow alerts yet: Twitch only shares follows with a signed-in app.
- Logos come from an image link; there's no upload.

### Privacy and safety
- No accounts, no cookies, no trackers. Settings live in your links and your browser.
- Twitch chat is read anonymously. Chat text is always shown as plain text.
- Error reports (Sentry) leave out your settings, chat messages and IP address.
